# Customers_Churn
Better Customers - Better Business

The project uses Python, Pandas, Excel, SQL, Power BI, and DAX to perform data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, customer segmentation, churn analysis, KPI calculation, and interactive dashboard development.


Customer Churn & Subscription Analytics is a data analytics project focused on understanding customer subscription behavior and identifying patterns related to customer churn.

The project includes **4 interactive Power BI dashboards** covering:

* **Customer Analysis** — customer demographics, locations, interests, and customer distribution.
* **Subscription Analysis** — subscription trends, tenure, monthly charges, and subscription performance.
* **Churn Analysis** — churned vs. active customers, churn trends, and churn patterns.
* **Churn Risk & Revenue Analysis** — high-risk customers, churn risk categories, complaints, escalations, and revenue at risk.

The dashboards use **interactive filters, slicers, KPI cards, charts, and tables** to provide a detailed view of customer and subscription performance.

The main objective is to identify **customers at risk of churn, understand churn-related patterns, and analyze revenue at risk** to support data-driven customer retention decisions.
